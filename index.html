Shorts Studio

Shorts Studio is a private tool that uploads and schedules YouTube Shorts for the creator's own channels, Hidden Engineering and Wild Mechanics.

It is not offered to the public. Only the channel owner signs in with Google, and the tool acts on the owner's own channels only: it uploads finished videos, sets their title, description, thumbnail and publish time, and reads view counts for a private dashboard.
